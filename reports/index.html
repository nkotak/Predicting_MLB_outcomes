Does the model know anything the market does not?

A simple mix of the market's chance and the model's chance was fitted on earlier seasons and scored on the next one. If the mix scores no better than the market alone, the model holds nothing the price does not already contain.

Closing price, 22,415 games: market alone 0.6715, market mixed with the model 0.6715, change +0.0000 (95% range +0.0000 to +0.0001), so the model adds nothing to this price that can be told apart from chance. The mix gives the model 5% of the weight.

Opening price, 4,858 games: market alone 0.6807, market mixed with the model 0.6795, change -0.0012 (95% range -0.0022 to -0.0001), so the model adds real information to this price. The mix gives the model 59% of the weight.

The learning agent (GRPO) is retrained before each season on earlier seasons only. In 9 of 9 seasons, no betting policy was proven better than not betting on the held-back season, so it passed on every game. Passing is a real result: it means the evidence did not support betting at these prices.

What the learning looked like: averaged over these seasons, the agent started out betting on 60% of its training games and ended up betting on 6%. On the held-back season, what it still wanted to bet on did not make money.

"Safety bar off" shows the bets the agent's learned policy would have placed if it were not required to prove itself first: 1,283 paper bets, 64.4% won, -1.3% return per unit staked (95% range -5.5% to 2.6%). The safety bar is what stopped those bets from being recommended.

How to read this page

No peeking. Every input for a game is built only from earlier games, and the model can only see an approved list of inputs. A test fails if any input changes when that game's own result is changed.

Calibration. When the model says 60%, it should happen about 60% of the time. The calibration error column shows how far off that is on average.

Why the market is hard to beat. Prices already reflect line-ups, injuries, weather and the money of professional bettors, and the bookmaker takes a cut of about 4% on top. This model now sees line-ups, injuries and weather too, so what is left is the judgement of those bettors.
